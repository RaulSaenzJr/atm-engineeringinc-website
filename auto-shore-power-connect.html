Featured Product

Auto Shore Power Connect

The ASP monitors shore power 24/7. If power to the vessel is lost, the ASP waits for it to return — then automatically re-energizes the panel's motorized breakers or contactors and reconnects the vessel to shore power, with no one aboard needing to flip a switch.

Available in AC- or DC-powered versions, with optional support for shore power converters and a ship AC bus monitor alarm, so it fits panels ranging from a single AC bus to a split-bus configuration.

Request a Consultation

How It Works

Set it to Auto, and forget it.

A Manual/Auto selector switch on the panel controls the ASP. In Manual mode, the unit stands by and does nothing. Switch it to Auto and the ASP energizes the panel's motorized breakers or contactors, then waits for the next power disruption — restoring shore power automatically every time it returns.

Use Cases

Applications

  • Power management system integration
  • Monitoring and maintaining continuous power to the vessel
  • Keeping battery chargers, refrigerators, and freezers powered while the vessel is unattended

What's in the Box

A complete, panel-ready unit.

Every ASP comes complete with controller, relays, fuses, and a switch with an engraved panel — sized to fit inside a switchboard, a cabinet, or a remote enclosure at just 6 × 4 × 3 inches (152 × 101 × 76 mm).

Configurations

The ASP Family

  • ASP-1 AC powered, no support for shore power converters
  • ASP-1-SC AC powered, with support for shore power converters
  • ASP-2 DC powered, no support for shore power converters
  • ASP-2-SC DC powered, with support for shore power converters
  • ASP-2-SC-AL DC powered, with support for shore power converters and a ship AC bus monitor alarm
