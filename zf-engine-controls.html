Featured Product

Electronic Propulsion Control

ATM Engineering installs and services ZF Marine Electronics SmartCommand and CruiseCommand electronic propulsion control systems — precision throttle and clutch control engineered to withstand the marine environment.

From a single-station center console to a multi-station yacht with flybridge and docking positions, we spec, install, and commission the right control head configuration for your vessel.

Request a Consultation
Factory-trained ZF Marine Electronics installer.

How It Works

One module. Every station.

A single SmartCommand module ties together throttle, clutch, and every helm station on the vessel — main helm, flybridge, or docking station — over a CAN bus network, with easy transfer of control from one station to the next.

Single-lever operation combines throttle and clutch into one intuitive motion, and a built-in warm-up mode protects the engine before you ever leave the dock.

Capabilities

Built for Real-World Helms

  • Multiple control station capability with easy station transfer
  • Warm-up mode to protect the engine before departure
  • Single-lever throttle and clutch operation
  • Built to withstand the harshest marine environments

What's Included

A complete, ready-to-install kit.

Each system ships as a complete kit — control heads, a junction/control box, and a pre-terminated wiring harness connecting throttle, clutch, and every station — ready for our team to install and commission on your vessel.

Configurations

Multiple Control Head Styles

ZF builds control heads in a range of styles — from wheel-mount dual-lever units to compact side-mount levers — so the helm keeps a clean, factory-finished look no matter the layout. We'll help you choose the right style for your vessel.
